# Want to Lead an AI Learning Community in Your College? Become an Ambassador

Learning AI alone is slow. Learning it with friends, in a room where someone is always ready to share what they just figured out, is much faster. That is why I want to tell you about the chapter program, which is looking for ambassadors to lead it in their colleges. Here is how it works, step by step.

## 1. What a Chapter Is

A chapter is a local community that meets regularly to learn, share and build together. Each chapter has a host or coordinator, and ambassadors from different colleges who keep the energy up.

Three cities are in the plan:

- Chennai
- Coimbatore
- Trichy (coming next)

Every chapter meets once every two weeks, offline or online, so nobody is left out because of distance.

Once you understand what a chapter is, the next question is who keeps it running. That is where the ambassador comes in.

## 2. What an Ambassador Does

An ambassador is the face of the chapter in their college. Every chapter needs at least one ambassador each month: someone who enjoys welcoming new people, getting a conversation started and making sure nobody sits alone.

As an ambassador, you will:

- **Invite and welcome** students from your college to every meetup
- **Help the host or coordinator** run the session
- **Pick up the snacks**
- **Encourage members** to join the talks, games and open source work

**The stipend is INR 500 to INR 2,000.**

## 3. What a Meetup Looks Like

Every meetup is 50 minutes at most. The goal is simple: make friends first, and learn along the way. Attendance is tracked for every session.

A session runs in this order:

1. A random fun challenge
2. An AI talk
3. Fun games
4. A talk on open source contributions

## 4. Certificates and Recognition

Once you are attending regularly, the hours start to add up, and so does the recognition.

- **Participation certificate:** Complete at least 50 hours of chapter activity, with a minimum of 5 hours in open source contribution.
- **Ambassador bonus:** Once an ambassador completes 50 hours of work, they receive an extra bonus on top of the stipend.
- **Milestone pick:** For every 100 hours of work, one member is picked for recognition.
- **Quarterly top performer:** Every quarter, one top performer is chosen across all chapters, who receives a certificate, a bonus and INR 5,000.

*The first evaluation happens at the end of three months.*

## 5. Why It Is Worth Your Time

Honestly, you get five things out of it:

- A **network of friends** who share your interest in AI
- Regular practice **speaking in front of a group**
- A record of **open source contributions** you can show later
- **Certificates, a stipend** and a shot at the quarterly prize
- An **AI community in your own college**

## 6. How to Apply

Once you have decided this is for you, the next step is a short message. Tell them three things:

1. Your college
2. The chapter you belong to
3. Why you would be a good ambassador

If you enjoy bringing people together, this is the place to start.

Every college has a few people who wait for someone else to start the conversation. If that is you, your college may end up with no chapter, and you may end up wondering why you stayed quiet.
